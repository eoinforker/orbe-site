Scope

Checked

  • Provenance. Every figure carries the source it came from, the account it was read from and the date it was read.
  • Freshness. Each source was judged against its own window: Podcast RSS feed, audience, public: fresh; Stripe, revenue, connected: fresh; Substack export, audience, uploaded: fresh; YouTube public data, audience, public: fresh.
  • Consent. Connected data was used only under the creator's live consent to connect.
  • Double counting. Where two sources describe the same money, only the more authoritative was counted and the other used to corroborate it.
  • Audience signals. Engagement against audience size, sudden audience changes and view spikes, from public and connected reads of @@seedadaplays, @seedadaletters, @seed-shed-arcade.

Not checked

  • Stripe charges against payouts (acct_SEED000001). Stripe payouts were not supplied for acct_SEED000001, so charges were not reconciled to money paid out.
  • AdSense against YouTube Analytics. Neither AdSense nor YouTube Analytics revenue was connected.
  • Public audience against connected (Audience). No account was read both publicly and through the creator's login within three days, so audience counts were not cross-checked.
  • Bank records. No bank statements or open-banking data were provided. Orbe did not see any bank account, so no figure here is confirmed against money in the bank.
  • Costs and profit. Orbe reads revenue only. Nothing in this report describes costs, margins or profit.
  • Tax. Tax returns, VAT registration and the tax treatment of any income were not checked.
  • Contracts. The contracts behind sponsorships, memberships and licences were not read. Uploaded invoices were taken as uploaded and checked only against payments Orbe could see.
  • Ownership and rights. Ownership of the channel, brand, domain, content and any music or footage rights was not checked beyond the creator's ability to log in to each connected account.
  • Identity. Orbe confirmed that whoever connected each account could log in to it. It did not check identity documents.
  • The future. Nothing here forecasts future revenue or audience.
  • Audience behind the login. No audience data was read through the creator's own login, so audience figures are public counts only.
  • Face dependence. Nobody declared how much the business depends on the creator appearing in it, and Orbe does not measure it.

Method

Freshness windows: YouTube public data fresh for 7 days, stale after 30 (YouTube API data must be refreshed or deleted within 30 days); Stripe fresh for 7 days, stale after 30 (Stripe is read live, so a week-old read is already behind); Substack export fresh for 30 days, stale after 90 (Substack data arrives as a manual export); Podcast RSS feed fresh for 7 days, stale after 30 (Feeds change with every episode).

Tolerances, freshness windows and risk thresholds are Orbe's provisional judgements, published so a reader can disagree with them, and will be replaced by measured distributions as verified records accumulate.

Revenue is gross, in GBP, by the month it belongs to. The trailing twelve months run Oct 2025 to Sep 2026; the current month is left out until complete.
