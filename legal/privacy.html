Orbe uses YouTube API Services

Orbe uses YouTube API Services to read channel information. By using Orbe's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we read, and why

  • Public statistics about your channel, to rate your work against creators your size.
  • With your consent, YouTube Analytics views, watch time, subscribers gained and estimated revenue, shown only to you and to the buyers you choose, one at a time.
  • With your consent, payout records from the accounts you connect, read only. Orbe cannot move your money or change anything in your accounts.

How long we keep it

Data read through YouTube API Services is kept for 30 days unless it is refreshed, and derived figures for 30 days. If you disconnect or revoke access, what we read is deleted within 30 days. The final periods will be set out here once the policy is approved.

Who sees it

You, and the buyers you name, one at a time. YouTube Analytics figures are never public. We never sell your data.

Your contact details and your profile

We never sell your contact details, and nobody gets a profile here unless they claim it. In plain English:

  • We never sell, rent or swap your email address or any other way to reach you. Not to brands, not to agencies, not to anyone.
  • Orbe can work out a rating for a public channel when someone asks, but there is no profile page for you on Orbe until you claim it yourself.
  • A team, such as a manager, a brand or a fund, sees only what you choose to share with it, one thing at a time, and you can take it back.
  • If you reserve a handle, we use your email only to check the handle is yours and to write to you about it.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can disconnect a source or delete your account at any time, or email hello@orbe.vc. You can also revoke Orbe's access to your Google account through Google's security settings page.

Cookies

One session cookie, to keep you signed in.
