Motion lab

Orbe's moving pieces, in Orbe colours and Orbe words. Each one names the page and moment it is for, and whether it is there yet.

In use means on a page now. A candidate is chosen for a moment but not built in yet.

  1. 01Stickers Die cut vinyl words with hard ink shadows: the top 1%, viral, followers, the charts, vanity views, and your size. In user6-a, the first panel. The wrong yardsticks: fling or tap them away until only your size is left. Also for the launch film and social posts.
  2. 02Stamp type A printed poster loop in the brand colours: four lines hop in, hold to be read, then scatter. CandidateSocial posts and the launch film. Shown in the brand book; never behind a figure.
  3. 03Reveal Two lines of type dissolve in on flat Paper, like weather. Candidater6-g, panel three. The one line a creator would post, arriving as the panel opens.

Parked, with no moment yet: Tiles, one sentence in the brand five that keeps reshuffling. r6-e, the chosen site, ends on its form on ink and has no closing line for it, so it waits until a page has one.

Taken out of the lab: Word trail (it needs a cursor, so it has nothing to do on a phone), Galaxy (floating screenshots of work), Holo (a foil identity card), Sphere (a globe of work), Code trail (torn analytics source) and Datamosh (falling colour blocks). Their files stay for the record.
