01

The mark. The Portal, then the word.

The Portal is a block with one corner cut flat, a round counter, and a channel rising through it, flaring into the counter and fine at both ends: a way in, and a line going up. Beside it, Orbe is lettered the way names are cut into stone: every stem narrows at its waist and flares to a flat cut end. Both are drawn as paths, never set in a font.

lockup.svg

The lockup: the Portal exactly as tall as the O, on the same line, then a gap of 0.42 of the word’s height. The dashed frame is the clear space, a third of the lockup’s height on every side. Nothing comes inside it.

The cut. In the header the word is cut once on load, 1.1 seconds: a chisel runs each stroke in writing order, then the flares open at the ends. The Portal never moves. Under reduced motion both are always still.

The Portal on its own

Small

Under 48px the small cut takes over: the channel runs even instead of tapering, so it stays open at 16px. It is chosen for you by size. The favicon is the small cut in Paper on a rounded Cobalt tile.

The lockup, one flat colour, on four grounds

Ink on Sky, the field.

The header size, 22px: both small cuts.

Always

  • Size the lockup by the word’s height (--wm-h) and keep its proportions and its gap.
  • Use the Portal alone where a square is needed: tabs, app icons, avatars.
  • One flat colour from the palette, the same for the mark and the word.

Never

  • Rotate it. The channel rises left to right, always.
  • Add effects: no shadow, glow, gradient, glass, bevel or 3D.
  • Outline it, or draw it as a stroke.
  • Stretch it, redraw it, or set “Orbe” in a font as a stand in.
  • Put it in a circle, seal or badge.

02

Colour. Sky and Ink do the work. The rest is art.

Warm Paper for reading, Sky for the field, Ink for type, Cobalt for the band and the action’s deep blue. Straw, Peony and Claret paint the product windows. Every value and ratio here is read from the live tokens.

The primaries

Papers, inks and accents

Product pairs: a field and its words

Each product has one pair. A window’s body takes it, and every ink, rule and focus ring inside follows. Ratios are words on field, computed here from the tokens (WCAG 2.2; body text needs 4.5 to 1).

Archetype pairs: one per kind of work

On the homepage’s instrument each kind of work brings its own field, words and the cell that is you.

The rules

Always

  • Accents are art. Straw, Peony, Claret and Ember paint fields, windows and pixels. They are never the colour of a sentence on Paper.
  • Ember is the single “you”. One small orb on the closing band holds a creator’s own place. Ember appears nowhere else on a screen.
  • Words on a field use its pair. If a pair isn’t listed above, it hasn’t been checked.

Never

  • No purple, at any strength, anywhere: not in a gradient, a hover or a chart.
  • No glows. Depth is one soft stack of shadows, never a coloured halo.
  • No red or green for a rating. A rating measures; it does not judge.

05

Texture and motion. Explain once, then stop.

Paper has grain and fields have light, so the screen feels made rather than rendered. Motion shows how something works one time and then holds still.

Paper grain and gradient light

Flat Sky
Sky, lit, with grit
Straw, lit
Cobalt, the band

The whole page carries one fixed grain, multiplied at a few percent; it is off under reduced transparency. A field is lit from the top left and deepens to the lower right, with a coarser grit under the words. Never a glossy highlight.

The lattice and pixel objects

Move your pointer here. The trail lands on the 11.28px lattice.

One object per kind of work, in three colours, shaded by omission: a shadowed cell is left out, never darkened. When it changes, each column’s cells travel straight up or down, once. No faces, no characters.

The one easing

cubic-bezier(0.6, 0.04, 0.3, 1)

A slow start, a long settle and no overshoot. Nothing bounces: not a figure, not a fee, not a window. CSS uses var(--ob-ease); scripts use ease() from ease.js.

What moves, and when it stops

  • The wordmarkCut once in the header on load, then still.
  • A figureCounts to its value once when it first shows.
  • A pixel objectRises into place up its columns once; travels once when its word changes.
  • The pointer trailFollows a mouse and fades in 0.9s. Off on touch. No frame runs once the last cell has gone.
  • The footer markThe one loop. Cuts, fills and turns through the six product pairs, only while it is on screen.

Reduced motion on this device: off

Under reduced motion every piece is drawn settled: the mark still, the trail off, the footer on one still frame, smooth scrolling off, every change instant.

06

Voice. A friend who is very good with numbers.

Warm first, exact always, short. One person, second person, British English. No dashes, no exclamation marks in product copy, no hype.

Build your goliath. Somewhere tonight someone is uploading at 3am to a channel nobody has found yet. That is how every enormous thing starts.

Free for creators, for good. Made by people who refresh the same pages you do.

Creator first

Their work, then their number, then what it means for them. Talk about what they are building; the deal is never the point.

Honest about size

“Creators your size” is the whole idea. Zero is typical, and that’s how big things get built. Never “only”, “just” or “small” beside someone’s numbers.

Money only when it moves

Free for creators, for good. Orbe earns when money moves, and says plainly who pays. Read from payouts, not estimates.

Made-up labels

Every example creator is invented and says so: “A made-up creator, rated live by the Orbe engine.” “Independently calculated by Orbe.”

In practice

  • SayYou beat 93 in 100 channels your size.
  • NotTop 7%! You’re crushing it!
  • SayZero is typical. Most weeks are, and that’s how big things get built.
  • NotYour score is 0. Try posting more consistently.
  • SayYouTube estimated £412. AdSense paid £388. Your record uses £388.
  • NotVerified revenue, powered by our proprietary pipeline.

Words we use

work, uploads, letters, episodes, photos, your size, typical, personal best, record, paid, read, building

Words we don’t

content, unlock, hustle, grind, journey, monetise, funnel, users, AI-powered
