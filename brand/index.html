01 Colour

Blue leads. Pink is the dawn.

A sky field with ink type, a cobalt action and an ember spark, on warm paper. Blue is the colour people already trust with money, so the fun reads as confidence. Pink stays in the paintings and is never a field beside cobalt.

You are looking at the pink alternative. The rules here are written for blue, the scheme that ships: in pink, read peony for sky, ink for the cobalt action and claret for the deep panel.

Contrast, computed (WCAG 2.2)

Text needs 4.5:1 for AA and 7:1 for AAA. Large text (24px, or 19px bold) needs 3:1 and 4.5:1. A shape that carries meaning needs 3:1. Computed live from the hex values.

Always

  • One field per screen. Sky is the field; paper is where people read and products live.
  • Ink for type on sky, paper and ember. Cobalt for the one action and links.
  • Ember as a spark: one brush stroke under a figure. On a light field it never carries meaning alone.
  • Functional colours only for states in the product, always with words beside them.

Never

  • Never colour a rating red or green. A rating measures; it does not judge.
  • Never put paper or white text on sky or peony, or body text in cobalt on sky.
  • Never set peony beside cobalt as two fields.
  • Never use gradients, glows, purple or a second field on one screen. No seals, badges or ticks.
  • Never recolour a creator's work to match the brand.

03 What Orbe draws

Square for numbers. Paint for people.

Three pieces, each already on a page or chosen for one. Nothing is drawn that does not count something or hold someone's work.

  • Counting marks: square windows with an ink bar, the band rule with a square marker, the reach track out of 100, the struck verdict.
  • People's marks: paper folders with a named tab, painted ground, a small dark tooltip naming the work: upload the marsh at 4am
  • Their work is shown as they made it, cropped to fill, never filtered or painted over. Orbe's paintings stand in only where there is no work, and say they are made up.
  • Parked: Tiles waits in the motion lab until a page has a closing line for it.
