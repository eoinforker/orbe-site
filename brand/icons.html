Brand book / Icons

Icons, found by shape.

The same 25 Orbe glyphs drawn by six open icon sets, at 16, 20 and 24 pixels, on Orbe's papers, field, cobalt and ink. Then the one Orbe uses, and why.

Phosphor.

Regular above 18 pixels, bold at 18 and under. MIT, vendored in assets/icons/ with icons.js.

  1. It holds the brand's line. Orbe draws with a 1.5px line. Phosphor regular is a 16 unit stroke on a 256 grid, 1.5px at 24; bold is 24 units, 1.5px at 16. One rule keeps every icon on the line at every size. Lucide and Tabler are fixed 2px strokes; Iconoir thins to 1px at 16.
  2. It has every glyph we need, including ours. Pound, hand with coins, plugs connected, broadcast, microphone, handshake: all 25 without a second set or a drawn stand-in.
  3. Its corners are soft. Rounded joins sit with Schibsted Grotesk and "round for people"; Carbon's square ends read as an enterprise console, Font Awesome's solid fills shout and need an attribution line.
  4. Filled paths, one colour. Every glyph is a path in currentColor, so it takes the text's colour on paper, sky, cobalt and ink with no stroke settings to carry.
16px, bold: inside buttons, rows and menus.
20px, regular: icon buttons, a drop zone's mark.
24px, regular: the one mark on an empty state.

The candidates

From the design.how icon libraries directory (17 sets), the six that are open, drawn for interface sizes and cover Orbe's glyphs. Each as published on npm, untouched, scaled to the size shown.

On View

Left out, and why

Simple Icons
Brand logos only. Platform names are written as words on Orbe.
Iconify
An index of other sets, not a set.
Material Symbols
Reads as Google; its variable axes are more than we need.
Fluent UI
Reads as Microsoft; tuned for Windows sizes.
Octicons
GitHub's own nouns; no pound, podcast or payout.
Radix Icons
Drawn for 15px only, and a small set for editors; no microphone, pound or payout.
Heroicons
About 320 glyphs, well drawn, but no handshake, payout or broadcast, and only one stroke weight per size.
Bootstrap, Ionicons, MingCute
Complete, but a second rounded set with nothing Phosphor lacks.
Hugeicons
The free tier ships as JavaScript data, not SVG files, and the full set is paid.
Pixelarticons
Not in the directory. Crisp only at multiples of 24, and the pixels are the creatures' job, not the interface's.
