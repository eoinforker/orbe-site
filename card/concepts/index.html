The card, round two.

The first card was a template that led with a number. This round is a family of moments: a personal best minutes after posting, the rating told through your #1 stat, your year, and moving up a size. Each says one sentence a creator would say out loud, backed by one figure, and frames the creator's own work.

Every creator and every picture here is made up: the five creators come from the site's examples, rated live by the Orbe engine, and the artwork was drawn for this board. Set in the Masthead type with the A3 Carved wordmark.

What the cards that spread have in common

  1. They say who you are, not what happened. Wrapped's "top 1% of listeners" and Duolingo's percentile rank are identity statements; Duolingo found a second identity hook raised share rates.
  2. One image that works without scrolling. Duolingo chose a condensed card that reads like a picture, over a page of stats.
  3. Your own work is the hero. Strava's stats sticker sits on your own photo; Spotify's promo cards carry the artist's own artwork.
  4. The brand's own form carries the data. Wrapped 2024 used its typeface as the graphic; Strava's year used the route line.
  5. Celebrate a best, even in a bad week. Leetify's personal bests and achievements are its most shared unit.

Chosen: the lap

The O of Orbe goes round as a measure. One lap is the yardstick the card names, said once in italic: your usual upload, your old best, or a hundred creators your size. The stroke goes round as far as you went and lands on the ball; past one lap the O closes and carries on inside it, short of one it stays open with a hairline for the rest. The creator's own work sits in the window, so the card frames their thumbnail, cover, art or photo, and with no picture the window holds the work's title. It is what Orbe does, drawn: every lap measured against your own last one.

Runner-up: the sticker

The creator's work is the whole card and Orbe is a paper sticker on it, carrying the same sentence, figure and a small live lap. It is the most native in a feed and the easiest for a creator to say yes to; it gives up the drawing that makes the lap Orbe's own.

Round one: nine concepts

Each drawn at story and square size, then judged: would a YouTuber with 400k subscribers post it to their story, would a Substack writer post it to Notes, would it sit next to a Wrapped card?

01

The lap Chosen

Your usual work is one lap of the Orbe O; the stroke goes round as far as you went, and your work sits in the window.

Verdict. Chosen. The only concept that is Orbe's own and explains something: one lap is the yardstick, so 1.8× your usual is the O and four fifths more. The risk is that the small key has to be read for the measure to land.

02

The sticker Runner-up

Your work is the whole card and Orbe is a paper sticker on it, like a stats sticker on a run photo.

Verdict. Runner-up. The most native thing in a feed, because the creator's own thumbnail is the picture. But the Orbe idea shrinks to a small mark, and the panel covers the work.

03

The contact sheet

Your last twelve as a contact sheet, with the best one ringed the way an editor marks the frame to print.

Verdict. The best picture of 'against your own last twelve', and very photographic. But it needs twelve pictures, and it only explains one moment.

04

The sentence

The sentence is the picture, set huge, with your work dropped into it like a word.

Verdict. The most energy in a feed. But type as the graphic is Wrapped's 2024 move, and the work shrinks to a stamp.

05

The wall label

Your work hangs full size and Orbe is the gallery label beside it.

Verdict. Calm and native, but any brand could make it. There is no Orbe idea in it.

06

Then and now

Your old best and your new one side by side, each drawn at the size of its number.

Verdict. Clear, but static: two covers side by side read as a comparison advert.

07

The hundred

A hundred creators your size, ranked by your best stat, with you as the ball.

Verdict. Honest about 'creators your size', but it is a bar chart: the dashboard look we ban.

08

Up a size

The size bands as nested rings; a milestone is the ball moving out a ring.

Verdict. Concentric rings are a solar-system cliché. The useful part, winding outward, became the lap's size moment.

09

The trace

Your last twelve drawn as one line over your work, landing on the ball at the newest.

Verdict. A line chart over art. It reads as analytics, not a moment.
