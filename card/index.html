Your card.

Every moment comes as a card made to be posted: a personal best minutes after you post, your rating, your year, moving up a size. One sentence, your work and your link.

  1. The lap is the O of Orbe. One lap is the yardstick the card names: your usual work, your old best, or a hundred creators your size.
  2. The ball lands where you got to. Past one lap the O closes and the stroke carries on inside it; short of one it stays open.
  3. The window holds your own work: your thumbnail, cover, art or photo. Without one it holds the title.
  4. The link brings anyone who sees it to orbe.vc/@you, where the maths is published.
