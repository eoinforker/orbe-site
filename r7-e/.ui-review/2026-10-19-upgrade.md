# r7-e upgrade, UI review (19 October 2026)

Is it ready to show? Yes, with one fix still open. Ready to ship: yes, with fixes (blocking 0, should-fix 1, notes 3).

## Review record
- Source: site/r7-e served locally on port 8717. Tool: Playwright Chromium (/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194). Widths 390 (DPR 2, touch), 1440, 1920. Pink and blue. Reduced motion on and off.
- Purpose (assumed): rate a made-up creator against its size, show the money in full, and get a handle reserved.
- Lint: `node scripts/check-site.mjs site/r7-e` exited 0 with 0 findings.

## Probes
- Console errors: none in the 12 runs (3 widths, 2 schemes, 2 motion settings). Horizontal scroll: none.
- Keyboard: Tab goes skip link, nav, hero CTAs, the five kind buttons, Copy, three sliders, buyer access, handle, kind, submit, footer links, scheme. Every control shows a 2px ring (shots/focus-1440-9.png). The range thumbs and form fields draw their rings through the thumb pseudo-element or `:focus-within`.
- Touch targets at 390: none under 40px.
- Empty submit puts focus on the handle field and says "Type the handle you want first."
- Lab at its extremes (fewest subscribers, most views): views now stop at the subscriber count, so it reads 100% and 91 in 100, not 500000% (shots/m-lab-extreme.png).
- Motion: one easing family is in use (ease-out-quad 45, the nav curve 4). No keyframes. After the page settles, `document.getAnimations()` returns 0. The hero moon waxes linearly over 900ms after a 600ms delay (shots/motion-strip.png).

## Findings
- should-fix 1. The page is long: 9,048px at 1440 (10.1 screens) and 11,900 at 390 (14.1), against the spec's 5 to 6 and 7 to 8. The Times and the money section are most of the excess. Fix: fold the line you post into the Ink band, or cut the Times to three stories. This is a product call (see below).
- note 1. Small moons (48px, 4px pitch) read as textured discs more than as phases. The fee moons were raised to 96px so that the 15% and 8% crescents read.
- note 2. Newsprint moons draw the lit part in ink, so the dark mass is the figure. This matches every other moon on the page, but a photo of the moon would show the reverse.
- note 3. Blue and pink are still set by a footer toggle. The default stays pink because r7-e was the pink version.
