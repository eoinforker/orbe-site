Orbe, drawn: four lettering concepts

Designer A. Each concept is one idea carried through all four letters, drawn as paths on a 1000 unit em, not typed from a font. No winner is chosen here. Shown at 240, 48, 24 and 16 px on paper and ink, in the nav and on a rating card.

A1 Handed on
A2 One angle
A3 Carved
A4 Orbs

A1 Handed on

Each letter hands a stroke to the next: the r reaches back into the O, its ball comes to rest on the b, and the b and e share one stroke, so the name is drawn as one connected business.

A3 Carved

Glyphic lettering in the tradition of cut inscriptions and the City of London's street names: every stroke is waisted and flares to a crisp end, so the word reads carved, not printed.
