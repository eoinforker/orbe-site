Orbe identity, d-type. Options for the founder, nothing chosen or swapped yet.

Nine typeface systems on the real hero and rating card

Each option is the r5-4 page copied verbatim and restyled by tokens only, with the current wordmark, real copy and the live rating engine. Every face is open licence (SIL OFL 1.1, checked in each package) and self-hostable; files and licences are in fonts/. Ranked best first.

  • Figures are the brand. Big numbers (+0.64, £142,300) set in the display face with proportional lining figures; tabular only in columns and live counters. Tabular figures at 128 px open ugly gaps around the 1.
  • Mono only for addresses. orbe.vc/@handle and source dates stay mono. Scores, weights and percentiles move to the sans with tabular figures: the current Paper Mono labels read as a terminal, not a ledger.
  • One serif, used twice. The italic carries the turn in each headline and the band name; the roman carries the plain-English sentence on the card. The serif must survive both 84 px and 21 px.

1

Masthead

Display and UI Schibsted Grotesk Serif Newsreader (opsz, roman and italic) Figures and mono Commit Mono (handles only); Schibsted tabular for figures

The pick to refine. A grotesk with a newsroom's backbone, the best open italic for the second line, and figures with real weight at £142,300. Distinct without being loud; sits right beside the serif wordmark.

Rating card at 1440 and 390
2

Ledger

Display and UI Inter (Display optical size) Serif Newsreader Figures and mono Commit Mono (handles); Inter tabular for figures

Faultless and calm, the safest financial-grade answer; it loses on character, because Inter is the face of a thousand dashboards.

Rating card at 1440 and 390
3

Broadsheet

Display and UI Newsreader (headlines and big figures) Serif Inter (UI and body) Figures and mono Geist Mono (handles)

The most editorial and the most ownable; a serif +0.64 feels like a published result. Risk: reads as a publication before a financial product, and the serif plus sign is thin.

Rating card at 1440 and 390
4

Schibsted and Literata

Display and UI Schibsted Grotesk Serif Literata (opsz) Figures and mono Fragment Mono

Strong figures and a sturdy text serif on the card, but the Literata italic is bookish and too quiet in the H1.

Rating card at 1440 and 390

Geist and Source Serif

Display and UI Geist Serif Source Serif 4 (opsz) Figures and mono Geist Mono

Crisp at UI size and a good italic, but Geist reads as Vercel, and the mono labels drift towards a developer tool.

Timeless, figures fixed

Display and UI Timeless Sans (current) Serif Timeless Serif (current) Figures and mono Paper Mono for handles only; Timeless Sans tabular for figures

Proof that half the problem is routing, not faces: moving every figure out of mono lifts the card. Still a bespoke non-OFL licence, and the sans is pleasant rather than distinctive.

Plex

Well made and coherent, but it is IBM's voice; light weights look soft and corporate.

Instrument

The 2024 startup default. The italic H1 is lovely, but Instrument Serif is a display face and collapses as card text.

Fraunces

Fashion and food-magazine energy: hairline plus sign, Didone figures, a wide mono that shouts. Not a ledger.
