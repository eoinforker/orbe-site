Orbe identity · designer B · drawn grotesque

Three sans wordmarks

Three different ideas, each drawn as lettering on a pixel grid, not typed from a font. Every mark is shown at 240, 48 and 16 px on paper and ink, in the nav and on a rating card. No winner is picked here.

B1

Nest

The counter of the capital O is the exact outline of the lowercase e, so the capital is drawn around its own lowercase: a letter measured against its own size, the way Orbe measures a creator.

B2

Forward

A dark, condensed oblique in which every cut, stem tops, the r terminal and the e terminal, lies on one 10 degree line, so the word reads as a single forward movement: money moving into creator businesses.

  • Drawn upright with vertical terminals, then sheared 10 degrees, so every cut is parallel by construction, not by eye.
  • Squarer rounds (k 0.66), stems 150, counters 140 wide, tight fit. The e is open below the bar, so it never fills in at 16 px.

Module

Every stem, every counter and every gap between letters is exactly one module wide, so the mark is measured in its own unit and nothing else: black and white in perfect, even step.

  • Module 150 units, cap height 5 modules, every letter 3 modules wide, every gap 1 module at its narrowest.
