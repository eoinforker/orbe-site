Identity, designer C: symbol plus wordmark

Three symbols for Orbe

Each concept is a symbol and a drawn wordmark, shown at 240, 48 and 16px on paper and ink, as a favicon, in the nav and on a rating card. Numbered, not ranked. Every symbol is built from circles and straight lines with exact geometry, in one flat colour.

C1 Level

A big orb and a small one held level on one beam, because the fulcrum sits exactly where their sizes balance: every creator measured fairly against their own size.

C2 In proportion

Two vessels of different sizes filled to the same proportion of themselves: a score is how full you are, measured against your own size, not against someone bigger.

C3 One slope

Orbs of every size settle between one baseline and one rising line, each exactly where its own size puts it: the same fair slope holds the smallest creator and the largest, like a taper gauge.

Level

Honest note. Closest to the bar: the idea reads in a second and survives at 16px, but a balance is a known sign, so its originality rests on the true physics and the drawing, not on surprise.

Construction

Discs of radius 100 and 50 on a 34 unit beam. The fulcrum is the real centre of mass, computed with area as weight and the beam included, so the mark is in balance, not just drawn level. Wordmark: a drawn geometric lowercase, 112 unit stems against 94 unit horizontals so the rounds look even.

C2

In proportion

Two vessels of different sizes filled to the same proportion of themselves: a score is how full you are, measured against your own size, not against someone bigger.

Honest note. Clever and quiet, but it needs half a second of thought; at a glance it can read as two bowls or two half moons.

Construction

The small vessel is an exact 60 percent copy of the large one, ring weight and gap included; both sit on one baseline and are filled to 58 percent of their own height, so their levels differ but their proportion does not. Wordmark: a bold old style serif recut from Libre Caslon Text, the b ascender trimmed to cap height and the letters respaced for display.

C3

One slope

Orbs of every size settle between one baseline and one rising line, each exactly where its own size puts it: the same fair slope holds the smallest creator and the largest, like a taper gauge.

Honest note. Falls short of the bar: it reads first as a growth chart, the fairness needs a sentence, and at nav size it can pass for a slice of something.

Construction

A level baseline and a line rising at 24 degrees, both 18 units. Each disc is tangent to both inner edges, so each is 1.52 times the last, the only ratio that angle allows, then inset 6 units so light runs round it. Wordmark: open capitals cut from Inter Tight Medium with wide, even spacing.
