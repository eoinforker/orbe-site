  • fair_offer.txt

    Is this sponsorship offer fair for my size?

    Price it on what your work reaches, not your follower count. Check the offer against a fair range for your size, and get a counter to send.

  • equity.txt

    Should I take equity instead of cash for a brand deal?

    Usually not instead; sometimes as well. Judge the cash on its own, then see what the equity has to make up. Illustrative, not advice.

  • your_video.txt

    Who owns my video after a brand deal?

    Usually you do. The brand gets a licence: what it may use, where, and until when. Check what your deal allows.
