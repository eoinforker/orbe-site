The five terms to find. In plain English.

Usage rights
What the brand may do with your work once it is made. Without them, a brand can usually share your post but not reuse it elsewhere.
Whitelisting
Ads run from your account, under your name. It is you speaking for the brand to people who do not follow you, so it is priced on top and should end on a date.
Paid use
Money behind your video, as an ad or a boosted post from the brand's account. Organic means unpaid posts only.
Territory
Where in the world the brand may use it. Worldwide is worth more than one country.
Expiry
The last day the brand may use it. After that, more use is a renewal, and you can charge for it.

Common questions. Short answers.

Who owns my video after a brand deal?

Usually you do. A typical brand deal gives the brand a licence: permission to use your video in set ways, in set places, until a set date. Ownership moves only if your contract assigns your copyright to them, and in the UK an assignment of copyright has to be in writing and signed by you (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 90(3)). If you made the video as an employee, different rules apply. This is general information, not legal advice.

What are usage rights?

The terms that say what a brand may do with your work: which channels, whether it can put money behind it, where in the world, and until when. They are often worth more than the post itself, so price them.

What is whitelisting?

The brand runs ads from your account, under your name, usually with access you grant on the platform. It reads as you speaking, so it is worth more than the brand reposting your video, and it should be in the contract with an end date.

What is paid use?

The brand puts money behind your video, as an ad or a boosted post from its own account. Organic use means unpaid posts and reposts only. If your deal says organic, paid use needs a new licence and a new fee.

What happens when usage rights expire?

After the end date the brand must stop new use of your video. If it wants to keep going, that is a renewal, and you can charge for it. Put the end date in your diary.
