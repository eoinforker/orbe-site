Assets used by site/lab/orb

white_studio_05_1k.hdr
  "White Studio 05" by Grzegorz Wronkowski, from Poly Haven
  https://polyhaven.com/a/white_studio_05
  Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Used unchanged (1k Radiance HDR) as the studio light and reflections.

ground-w05.hdr
  Made for Orbe. The light reaching the paper with the orb on it, divided by the
  light reaching it without, rendered top down in Blender 4.2 Cycles (shadow
  catcher pass, 256 samples, denoised) from the HDRI above and the same glass,
  liquid and ring as the page. Encoded as Radiance RGBE.

orb-still.webp
  Made for Orbe. The same scene rendered in Blender 4.2 Cycles for browsers
  without WebGL 2 and for people who ask for reduced motion.
