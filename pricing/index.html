Fair questions. Straight answers.

Will the rating always be free?

Yes. The rating, Your Orbe, Podcasts, the weekly refresh and the weekly note are free for every creator, for good. The optional plan for heavy use adds room; it never locks the rating.

Who pays the sponsorship fee?

The brand, on top of your price, and only when the sponsorship completes. You keep 100% of what you charge.

Why is the heavy-use price provisional?

Because it isn’t on sale yet. £10 a month is our provisional price for heavy use. We’ll confirm it before anyone pays.

Why do teams pay when creators don’t?

Managers, brands and funds use Orbe to make money decisions about creators, so they pay for the tools. Creators choose what each team can see, and can take it back.

What if my business doesn’t sell?

You pay nothing. The seller’s fee comes out of the sale, only when it completes.

Will you sell my details, or list me without asking?

No. We never sell your contact details, and nobody gets a profile here unless they claim it. A team sees only what you choose to share with it, and you can take it back.

Does the Data API sell my data?

No. Connected data is never sold or pooled. The API serves ratings creators have chosen to make public, and benchmarks built only from consented, anonymised totals.
