Free, for good. The Chartable replacement.

Your podcast, measured.

When Chartable closed, independent shows lost their prefix, their history and their smart links. Orbe's version counts downloads the same way on every host and tells you which promos worked. About five minutes to set up.

What works today
Live now
Feed reading, your exact prefix with your host's steps, the podcast:txt tag, the prefix check, the smart link rules and the plausibility model: the podcast module's own code.
In your browser
Your feed is read and checked in this browser. The dashboard runs Orbe's real counter, charts, promo and plausibility code over labelled sample listening, on your own episodes.
With the API
The prefix at m.orbe.vc counts real downloads (IAB-style, no IP addresses kept), your own dashboard fills in daily, smart links go live at orbe.vc/l/, charts are read every day, and Orbe verifies ownership.
