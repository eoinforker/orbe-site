    AppWhere the link sends them

    How it picks the app

    In order: an app the listener chose (?app=), the app they chose last time on this device (one functional cookie holding only the app's name), Apple Podcasts on Apple devices, and otherwise a plain page listing every app. A wrong app is worse than one tap, so Android and desktop get the page.

    Plausibility check

    For buyers, brands and your own peace of mind

    Is a download claim consistent with what can be seen in public? The model never accuses: each flag says what was compared, the numbers on both sides, and an innocent reading where there is one. Every threshold is provisional until Orbe has measured and host-reported numbers side by side.
