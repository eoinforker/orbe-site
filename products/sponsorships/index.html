Why 15% and 5% against beehiiv, Agentio, SparkLoop, Passionfroot

A refunded booking earns Orbe nothing; a 60% pro rata payout earns 60% of the fee. 15% sits under the 20% that beehiiv's Ad Network, Agentio and SparkLoop take and level with Passionfroot's network fee; 5% on direct deals matches Passionfroot's creator-sourced fee.
