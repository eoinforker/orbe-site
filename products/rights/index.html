The record

Orbe Rights Record v0

Every change is an event: who did it, when, and the SHA-256 hash of the event before. Change one fee anywhere and every hash after it stops matching. Revoking is an event too, so nothing is ever edited away.

The format is open: works, chain of title, the creator's consent scopes, usage grants and their events, in plain JSON. Anyone can read or write it without Orbe.

Imported files are read on this device and never sent anywhere.
