The tail clause

Why a sale after an introduction still owes the fees

Before any introduction, the seller (in the success fee agreement) and the buyer (in the buyer terms) agree that a sale between them, or anyone connected with them, of the business or any part of it, completing within months of Orbe's introduction owes Orbe's fees as if it had completed through Orbe.

  • Tied to one introduction, not to any contact. The end date is recorded and shown to both sides.
  • A sale before the introduction, after the tail, or between parties Orbe never introduced owes nothing.
  • Fees use the schedule each side accepted, never a later one. A minority stake is referred, not invoiced. A whole-company share sale goes to a lawyer first.
  • 24 months is the top of the 12 to 24 months common in broker agreements, because creator deals are slow. A UK lawyer still has to confirm it is enforceable and fair.
