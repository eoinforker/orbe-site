The rules. What Orbe Fix never does.

  • Never changes anything without you. Every swap waits for your tap. Titles can run on their own only if you turn that on for a channel; thumbnails always wait for you.
  • Never misleads. Options that add numbers, drift off the video's subject or shout in capitals are held back before you see them.
  • Never touches a sponsored upload unless the brand agrees.
  • Never charges without lift. Undo any change in one tap, at any time, with no fee.
