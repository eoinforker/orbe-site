# r6-c: The lettering studio

Led by Smith & Diction: a black field where lettering is the only art, and one brand colour used sparingly, with one full panel in it as the moment of contrast. Pink is the default, Blue is the alternative, and they switch with ?c=blue, ?c=pink or the small toggle in the corner. The page has five panels. (1) The Carved wordmark at poster size, then the headline set as a lockup in three hands: "YOUR WORK," in 900-weight capitals, "rated" in Newsreader light italic in the brand colour beside a ruled mono "AGAINST / CREATORS" banner, then "YOUR SIZE." Under it run two reels of lettering for every kind of creator, and one form to reserve a handle. (2) "ZERO IS TYPICAL.": the kinds of work are tabs, each set in its own hand, and the rating is set as a piece of lettering. The creator's name sits in a ruled banner over large serif numerals that count up from zero. The decimal point is a ball in the brand colour, with the band word in swash italic and the size band ruled underneath. Every figure comes from the engine. (3) The one panel in the brand colour, with fine grain: "WORTH posting." with the real card drawn by the product's own renderer (ink theme, its ball recoloured to the brand), then "read from payouts, not estimates". Sponsorship fees are lettered as 15% or 5%, paid by the brand, and a fair sale is "When you want one." (4) The second door: "BUYERS investors LENDERS", then "Creator businesses, read from payouts.": access is free and vetted, and investments go through an authorised partner. (5) "CREATORS never pay." and "Orbe earns a percentage when money moves.", with the signature moment: your handle lettered as a sign. It is a double-ruled shop sign reading orbe.vc / @yourhandle that letters your handle as you type in either form. On reserve it turns into the brand colour and the handle is cut in, honestly captioned "Held once your email arrives". This suits Orbe because the product is a number creators want to post and a name they want to own. Setting both with a type designer's care makes them worth showing off, and the black space, ruled captions and published sub-scores keep the page calm enough for a buyer or a VC.
