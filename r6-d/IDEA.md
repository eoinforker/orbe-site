# r6-d: fal's colour, creatures you can drag

The direction is led by fal.ai. One flat brand field fills the first screen: fal's baby blue #9EE9FB with its deep blue #1F4BFF by default, or TypeSafe pink #F2879E with ink. The founder can switch between them with the Blue / Pink control in the corner, or with ?c=blue or ?c=pink. The type is enormous and tight: Schibsted Grotesk 800, line height 0.84 to 0.88, tracking down to -0.06em. fal's own motifs are rebuilt for Orbe. There are pixel creatures drawn live on canvas: lumpy bodies with swaying limbs, a lattice of plus signs inside and a one-cell bright edge, stepping at about nine frames a second. There are two fat rounded bars crossing the hero, scattered pixel clusters that blink, and multicolour pixel stripes between panels. The per-kind colours (coral, lime, yellow, pink, sky) are secondary accents. They colour the creature, the inked headline word, the active size band and the money bars, while the brand colour leads.

Five panels, each with one job. 1. Hero: "Rated against [channels] your size." The word cycles through every kind of creator until you choose one, and there is one action, reserving a handle. 2. **Drag your size**, the signature moment, on a full-bleed deep blue panel. The example creator's real reach (25% for Field Recordings) is a dotted line across the engine's own size bands, drawn as dithered pixel columns. You drag the creator, a creature that grows as the audience grows, along that line. The giant readout rolls from 0.8× typical to 6.3× typical, and the copy says "Same 25%, 6.3× typical here", until you reach the creator's own band. There it reads "Rated here: +0.64 Average, reach beats 85 in 100 channels its size." One gesture shows why Orbe compares you only with creators your size. On first view the creature walks the scale once by itself, so people see it can be dragged. 3. The card and the money. The real product card (renderCard, ink theme) is dealt like a deck you tap through: rating, personal best, up a size, season. Then come three bold bars: Free; 15% or 5% on sponsorships, paid by the brand; a success fee from the proceeds when you sell. 4. The second door, on ink with a lime creature: Buy (free, vetted access), Invest (through an authorised partner), Lend (against records, not estimates), and "Request buyer access". 5. Close: "Creators never pay. Orbe earns a percentage when money moves.", the form again, and a giant wordmark in the style of fal's footer.

It fits Orbe because it feels made for streamers, YouTubers and podcasters: a playful creature, a card bold enough to post, controls a thumb wants to play with. Under the play, every figure comes from the engine and is labelled as an example or as the engine's starting baselines.
