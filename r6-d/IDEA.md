# r6-d: Drag your size, with Orbe's folk

The page runs on the brand tokens (`assets/brand/tokens.css`). Blue leads and pink is the alternative: the Blue / Pink toggle sets `data-scheme`, and `?c=blue|pink` still works. The fal pixel art is gone: no pixel creatures, crossing pills, pixel stripes, blinking clusters or giant footer wordmark. In its place Orbe has its own art, **the folk** (`folk.js`). Each one is cut from the Carved wordmark's O, stands on legs made from the wordmark's flared stems, and is lifted off the page on a hard ink shadow, with real paper grain over every panel. Each kind of creator carries one prop cut the same way. A channel wears a play flag, a stream has two antennae with the ember live dot, a newsletter wears an envelope, a podcast stands on a stem and base like a microphone, and for photography the O is the lens of a camera body. They are round because they are people ("square for numbers, round for people"). The O's counter is a real hole, and it can be filled like a glass. That glass is how the page measures.

Five panels, a different colour each: field, deep, paper, ink, then a landscape cut from paper.

1. **Field.** Folk of the chosen kind stand on a ruler in a line, smallest to biggest. A bracket marks the three who are "your size", and you are the one in colour. They grow up from the ruler on load and hop when the kind changes. Above the line-up sits "Rated against [channels] your size." On a phone the word runs through every kind once. A pause button sits by the chips, the old word fades within 180ms and the chip row fades at its edge. There is one action, Reserve my handle, and the handle is lower-cased with an honest "held" message.
2. **Deep (cobalt, or claret in pink): Drag your size.** Two folk stand side by side at the same size: the typical one for this size and the example creator. Each glass is filled to its reach and measured with level lines. When you drag, both grow, and the typical glass drains or fills as the size band changes. A line-up of all the sizes stands on the slider track. The giant figure (2.5× typical) is a figure, so it settles and never bounces. Off its own size, the verdict is struck through ("~~Looks like genius.~~ Wrong yardstick."). At its own size it reads "Rated here: +0.64 Average". The demo starts at the creator's own band. The drag follows the chosen kind. The per-kind weights from `mathsFor(kind)` and the full notice for the kind are shown.
3. **Paper: the verdict, which replaces the card.** "Zero is typical. Above it, you're ahead." The rating counts up once from zero. The creator walks the band ruler from 0 to where it stands. The sub-scores sit in a square window. "The line worth posting" sits in a folder in the kind's colour with Copy the line: a sentence a creator would actually post, built from their strongest part. Below that is the money, written in full in a window.
4. **Ink: buyers and lenders.** It carries the moat sentence and the terms in full (buyer's premium, diligence, lending), with no investing copy. A big folk peeks in at the terms.
5. **Cut-paper landscape.** Flat paper hills with inked edges, a carved O for a sun, and one folk of every kind standing on the ridge. Then "Creators never pay." and the footer.
