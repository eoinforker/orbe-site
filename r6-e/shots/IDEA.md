# r6-e: Wrapped, the story

The direction is Spotify Wrapped told as an Instagram story, built from four panels. Panel one is the story itself. On a phone it is the page: four full-screen moments with progress bars at the top, the creator's avatar and handle, and a reply bar at the bottom that is the real handle input, with the Blue/Pink switch sitting beside it where a story's share icons would be. The moments are [01] "You beat 86 in 100 newsletters your size", with the H1 above it and 100 cells filling to 86; [02] the Orbe Rating, +1.12 Good, with zero as typical, the five bands and the three part scores; [03] "The one you post", which is the real Orbe card drawn by the product's own renderer (story format, personal best of 319) with a link sticker, a working "Save the card" PNG download, and an "Up next: (?) @yourhandle" chip that leads into the last moment; and [04] "Your story is next.", where a story ring holds a question mark, your handle appears as you type it in any form on the page, and "You beat ? in 100 [newsletters, streamers...] your size" follows the kind you choose, above the reserve form. After the story come three panels: "Then, when money moves" (your record read from payouts; sponsorships at 15% through the network or 5% direct, paid by the brand; a fair sale with Orbe's fee from the proceeds), "The second door" for buyers, lenders and investors (free, vetted access, investments through an authorised partner, all five examples rated live), and the close, "Creators never pay. Orbe earns a percentage when money moves.", with the form again and the footer. Each palette has four grounds, used the same way: Blue has baby blue #9EE9FB, ink, deep blue #1F4BFF and paper; Pink has pink #F2879E, ink, claret #6F1726 and blush. The page wears the colour of the moment that is playing. Blue is the default. On desktop the same story plays itself inside a tilted phone beside the headline and form. The signature moment is the hand-off: the story ends on someone else's personal best, the next story in line is yours, and its number is a question mark you can only answer by reserving your handle. That fits Orbe because the product is a number worth posting, and every creator who posts one puts Orbe in front of their audience, which is full of other creators.
