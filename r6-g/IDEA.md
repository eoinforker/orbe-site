# r6-g: The archive

Every creator's work is art, so Orbe is drawn as a well-kept archive of it, painted. The page follows the founder's painterly references (flat-brush landscapes behind paper folders, "[01] Model artefacts", "Intimate, private, yours.") and Interfere's small dark tooltips naming each thing on show.

Round 6 fix: the paintings were the part that read as generated (turbulence-displaced ribbons and fractal grain), so they are now painted by `painter.js`, a canvas flat brush. Each stroke is a band of bristles that starts crisp, carries its paint with a second pigment riding on some bristles, and breaks into dry streaks where the paint runs out. Compositions are placed by hand, not scattered: two clouds, a thread of water, one stand of trees (trunks under crowns of strokes pushed out from the middle, one lit touch), three field bands and a clump of grass. The paper grain is a tile made once (fine tooth and a few fibres) laid over the whole page. No filters, no noise fields.

Panels change colour like pages: [01] the Sky (or Peony) field with a painted strip and a paper folder holding the H1, an example rating line (+0.64 Average, made up, Independently calculated by Orbe) and the handle form. [02] the field again, the drawer: a drawer of folders labelled with the engine's size bands; yours lifts, the flap tips and the creator's rating rises as a filed sheet set in type (no card): name, handle, the size band tooltip, the figure counting up once, three parts with ink bars exactly as long as the score, beside "the typical channel this size, rating 0.00, by definition". [03] ink: the one line a creator would post ("Zero is typical for channels my size. I'm at +0.64.") with one ember brush stroke under the figure, then the record and the money (sponsorships 15% or 5%; a sale's success fee 8% / 5% / 3%, £4,000 minimum). [04] the deep panel (Cobalt, or Claret): buy (premium, diligence) and lend, the data sentence and buyer access. [05] a painted dawn, the form again, the ink footer. Blue / Pink maps to the tokens' data-scheme.
