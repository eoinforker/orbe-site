# r6-g: The archive

Every creator's work is art, so Orbe is drawn as a well-kept archive of it, painted. The page follows the founder's painterly references (brush landscapes behind paper folders, "[01] Model artefacts", "Intimate, private, yours.") and the way design.interfere.com shows each thing in its own case with a small dark tooltip naming it. All the paintings are drawn in code: tapered SVG brush ribbons in layers (sky, far water, tree line, field, grass), roughened and dry-brushed with feTurbulence and displacement, with crayon grain on top. There are no image files. Type is Newsreader with bracket numbering, folder tabs mark the five panels, and Interfere-style pills ("artefact 00 [the archive, painted]", "Measured against 250k to 1M subscribers") label each thing on show. The five panels are: [01] Your work, the hero with one action, reserving a handle. [02] Your rating. [03] Your card, plus "Your record. Checked, private, yours." and the money: sponsorships at 15% or 5% paid by the brand, and a fair sale with the fee from the proceeds. [04] For buyers and backers: buy, invest through an authorised partner, or lend. [05] Free, for good, with the form again and the footer. The signature moment is in [02]. A drawer of folders, each labelled with one of the engine's size bands, sits shut. As it comes into view, your size's tab lifts, the front flap tips away, and your card rises out of it, filed in front of "the typical channel this size", which has a reach baseline and a rating of 0.00. Meanwhile your rating counts up from zero and the crayon bars draw in. Pick another kind of work and the card sinks back, is re-filed under that kind's size band and comes up again with its own painting. That is the whole idea of Orbe shown in one motion: you are measured only against the folder you belong in. Every figure comes live from the engine and is labelled as made up. A Blue (fal #9EE9FB with #1F4BFF) and Pink (TypeSafe #F2879E with ink) toggle repaints the archive's own skies and recolours the actions and the buyers' folder. The creators' paintings keep their own colours. This suits Orbe because it is warm and human for newsletter writers, photographers and podcasters, who see their work treated as something worth keeping. A VC still sees a method in it: published weights, size bands, a fee model where money moves and a separate door for buyers.
