# r6-g: The archive

Every creator's work is art, so Orbe is drawn as a well-kept archive of it, painted. Flat-brush landscapes (painted in code by `painter.js`) sit behind paper folders. The folders are Orbe's interface: each tab is set in Schibsted 600 and names what is filed in it ("Your work", "Channels, 250k to 1M", "Buyers and lenders", "Your handle"). No numbered brackets, no ruled kickers.

Panels: the Sky (or Peony) hero with the H1, the example rating (Field Recordings, read from the engine, counting in once over 700ms with the settle ease when first seen, instant under reduced motion), the handle form; on desktop the drawer's front folder sits closed in the field to the right as a preview. The drawer: folders labelled by size band; yours lifts, the flap tips and the rating rises as a filed sheet set in type, three parts with ink bars as long as the score, beside the typical neighbour at 0.00. Newsletters lead with paid conversion. Ink: the one line a creator would post, then the record and the money. The deep panel: buy and lend. A painted dawn, the form again ("Creators never pay."), the footer carrying the model line. Every figure comes from `examples.js`; none is written into the page.
